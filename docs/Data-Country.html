Description

Country represents an encoding-agnostic ISO 3166-1 country, identified as a 3-tuple (alpha-2, alpha-3, numeric) of its standard representations.

Note: numeric country codes are not numbers and should not be treated as such. Serialization to numbers is thus only provided for compatibility purposes.

Laziness

Evaluating a Country to WHNF evaluates it to normal form.
